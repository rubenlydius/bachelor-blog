After we created our styleboard and moodboard, we started with some first visual tryouts. We brought our lily into the browser with Three.js and tried several ways of making it appear and come to life:

- **Particles forming:** the flower appears first as points, then as a wireframe, and finally as the solid flower.
- **Flower with tracking boxes:** small selection boxes find and follow parts of the flower, for a more technical, "scanning" look.
- **Vertices growing into a flower:** particles trace the edges of the stem, leaves and petals before the flower fills in.

These tryouts showed us that the flower can appear in many ways, but the more technical looks (like the tracking boxes) felt too digital for the calm feeling we want.

## Coding roots

The second big part was the roots. Based on a growth test from a teammate, we built a page where roots grow from the spot where you shine a flashlight (or, for testing, the mouse). The roots first look like electricity and then turn green and natural. Small leaves and flowers grow along them, the whole system breathes, and after a while the roots dissolve into particles. I also added "fake lights", so we could test what happens when several people interact at once.

<Video src={rootsVideo} title="Roots growing from the cursor on a black background, with leaves and small flowers along them" caption="The coded version: roots growing as they react to the cursor." align="wide" />

## Sketching roots

Besides coding the roots, I also drew them myself, to see how they could look without the limits of the code. Drawing them helped me find the style direction I wanted: thin, flowing branches with small leaves along them, and flowers that grow out of the roots in a few places.

## Breathing effect

Since the main theme of the event is breath, we wanted to show the flowers as if they are breathing. We want to do that with the 3D lily, making it close and open in a slow rhythm, like a calm breath in and out. Below are some stages of opening and closing, showing how it would progress.

<Figure src={breathing1} alt="Five stages of the lily opening, from a closed bud to a fully open flower" align="wide" />

<Figure src={breathing2} alt="Four stages of a lily with long, wavy petals opening, from a closed bud to a fully open flower" caption="Stages of the lily opening and closing, like a slow breath." align="wide" />

These were just first tryouts to get started. We weren't 100% sure yet which effects we could add on top of the 3D, so we still need to experiment a bit more.
